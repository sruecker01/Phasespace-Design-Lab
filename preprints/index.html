About the collection

Each preprint has its own permanent public page, complete abstract, and downloadable PDF. Additional work will be added as individual manuscripts are prepared.

Authorship recognizes substantive intellectual contributions to research, including conceptualization, design, implementation, experimentation, analysis, and interpretation. Writing is one form of contribution, but not a prerequisite for recognition as an author. All named authors have had the opportunity to review and approve the work before publication.

The papers are preprints. Editorial work is limited to identifiable corrections and publication requirements, rather than rewriting authors’ arguments or voice. This collection is separate from the lab’s short Research Letters.
