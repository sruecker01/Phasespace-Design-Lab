Alternatively

Revisiting Common Designs

Stan Ruecker and Mike Cartwright

Drafted · Not yet published

Natalie Smith made a paving block out of lawn trimmings. She made more with wood chips, juniper and other materials the rest of us might have swept up. Concrete is a perfectly sensible thing to make a sidewalk out of. That still leaves a question: what might it feel like to walk on something else?

In Alternatively, Stan Ruecker and Mike Cartwright follow people who take a second look at familiar designs. An Illinois research team draws a sidewalk that can take up rainwater instead of sending it down the street. At a hospital entrance, a photocopied map becomes a set of directions for one person. In a New York plaza, a woman moves her chair about a foot before she sits down. A bench would have asked her to sit where somebody else thought she should.
