Privacy.

This notice describes how Phasespace Design Lab handles information submitted through the Phasespace Notes newsletter form. It does not describe every aspect of the third-party services that may operate the wider website.

Newsletter subscriptions

When you subscribe, your email address is processed using MailerLite, our newsletter service provider. MailerLite maintains subscription status and handles newsletter delivery, unsubscribe requests, and related administrative records.

Messages and preferences

Phasespace Notes is free and is normally sent approximately once a month.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter. You can also request help updating your subscription information or ask about removal of your information by writing to contact@phasespacedesignlab.org.

Newsletter analytics

MailerLite helps us track newsletter delivery and whether messages are opened. We use this information to understand readership and improve future issues.

Email-opening measurements are approximate and may be affected by the privacy settings and email software used by subscribers.

Third-party provider

The subscription form may load resources from MailerLite. Subscriber information is processed through MailerLite’s services.

For details of the provider’s own data-handling practices, please consult MailerLite’s privacy policy.

Questions

Questions about the laboratory’s handling of newsletter information can be sent to contact@phasespacedesignlab.org.
